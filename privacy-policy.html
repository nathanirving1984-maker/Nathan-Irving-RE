Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how this website (the "Site"), operated by Nathan Irving, a REALTOR® affiliated with Coldwell Banker Realty, collects, uses, and protects information you provide when you visit or submit a form on the Site.

Information We Collect

When you submit the Contact, Buyer Intake, Seller Intake, or Home Valuation forms on this Site, we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, email address, phone number, property address, and any details or messages you enter. We do not require you to create an account or provide payment information to use this Site.

Like most websites, we also automatically collect limited technical and usage data — such as pages visited, general location, device/browser type, and referral source — through Google Analytics.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through this Site is used to respond to your inquiry, provide the real estate services you requested (such as a market valuation, buyer search, or listing consultation), and to follow up with relevant information about buying or selling property in Marin County. Usage data is used only in aggregate to understand how visitors use the Site and to improve its content.

How Information Is Shared

Form submissions are processed through Formspree, a third-party form-processing service, and are also accessible to Nathan Irving and, where relevant to servicing your request, Coldwell Banker Realty's Greenbrae office. We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Information may be disclosed if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Nathan Irving, Coldwell Banker Realty, or others.

Cookies & Analytics

This Site uses Google Analytics, which places cookies on your device to help us understand aggregate visitor behavior. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using your browser settings or available opt-out browser extensions.

Data Retention & Security

We retain information submitted through this Site only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and to comply with applicable real estate recordkeeping obligations. We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect the information you provide, though no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices & California Privacy Rights

You may request to review, correct, or delete the personal information you've submitted through this Site by contacting Nathan directly using the information below. California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) to request access to or deletion of their personal information; we will respond to verifiable requests consistent with applicable law.

Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or a request regarding your information can be sent to Nathan Irving at nathan.irving@cbrealty.com or (415) 209-4864.
