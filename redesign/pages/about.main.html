About me

I'd rather know one town deeply than ten towns loosely.

I'm a Marin native, and I've spent the last decade around Hamilton and Pointe Marin. Novato is the town I know best, and it's where I live now.

I went to college at UNR and started my real estate career in Reno. Alongside that, I worked ballpark operations for the Reno Aces, where game day meant hundreds of small jobs that all had to land on time. A home sale feels a lot like that: inspections, disclosures, lenders, and deadlines that only work if someone is keeping track of all of them.

In 2026 I came home, joined Coldwell Banker Realty's Greenbrae office, and put my focus on Novato. I also work in property management, so I see the landlord and tenant side of local housing every week.

If you're thinking about buying or selling, or you're just curious what's happening on your street, I'm glad to help.
