Mortgage rates & Treasuries

What borrowing costs today

Mortgage rates follow the 10-year Treasury yield closely. When investors demand more to lend to the government, home loans get more expensive too. The gap between the two lines is the lender's spread.

Weekly mortgage averages: Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey (conforming loans, 20% down, strong credit). Daily Treasury yields: U.S. Treasury. Via FRED.
